After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

- Understand the principle and significance of torsion testing of engineering materials.
- Study the behaviour of circular shafts subjected to twisting moments (torque).
- Measure the angle of twist produced due to an applied torque.
- Plot and interpret the torque–twist relationship of the material.
- Determine the Modulus of Rigidity (Shear Modulus) of the material.
- Verify the torsion equation for circular shafts within the elastic limit.
- Understand the variation of shear stress across the cross-section of a shaft.
- Relate the experimental results to the design of shafts, axles, drive systems, and other torque-transmitting machine components.
