After performing this experiment, the learner will be able to:

1. Understand the principle and significance of torsion testing of engineering materials.
2. Study the behaviour of circular shafts subjected to twisting moments (torque).
3. Measure the angle of twist produced due to an applied torque.
4. Plot and interpret the torque–twist relationship of the material.
5. Determine the Modulus of Rigidity (Shear Modulus) of the material.
6. Verify the torsion equation for circular shafts within the elastic limit.
7. Understand the variation of shear stress across the cross-section of a shaft.
8. Relate the experimental results to the design of shafts, axles, drive systems, and other torque-transmitting machine components.
